eRruga Privacy Policy

Updated: September 9, 2026

Your privacy, explained clearly. Data is used for tickets, payments, scans, and safe trip operations.

Privacy with a clear purpose

eRruga collects only the data needed for accounts, tickets, payments, scans, and route operations. Every data point is tied to a specific service in the app.

Role-based access

Passengers, conductors, and companies do not see the same data. Each role sees only the information needed for work or travel.

Payments and cards

Card data is verified through Paysera. The app stores only the information needed to identify the payment method and payment history.

Operational traceability

Purchase time, travel date, and scan time are kept for ticket validation, misuse prevention, reports, and user support.

Privacy for passengers

This section explains how your profile, destination searches, tickets, and payments are stored.

Profile data

First name, last name, email, phone, and account creation date are used for identification, account access, and service communication.

Trip searches

Departure station, arrival station, and travel date are used to show direct routes or alternatives when no direct route exists.

Tickets

Ticket code, route, travel date, time, operator, price, and status are stored so the ticket can be displayed and validated when boarding.

Payments

Payment status, amount, currency, method, and reference are kept for purchase confirmation, history, and issue resolution.

How data is used

Accurate validation

The conductor can check whether a ticket is valid, which date it applies to, and whether it has already been scanned.

Your history

In your account you can see active tickets, past tickets, and payment methods you have registered.

Service notifications

Data may be used for important notifications about your ticket, payment, or service changes.

Your control

  • You can update your profile data in your account.
  • You can delete or change your payment method when available.
  • You can request clarification about the data stored for your tickets and payments.

How long is it kept?

Data is kept as long as needed to operate the service, verify tickets, prepare financial reports, provide security and support, and meet legal obligations. When it is no longer needed, it should be deleted or anonymized according to system rules.

Privacy questions?

Contact the eRruga team for access, updates, or clarification about your data.
